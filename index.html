GUALO: A Generalizable Uncertainty-Aware AI Agent for Logic Optimization

Jingxin Wang*, Sinian Zhang*, Yun Liang, Yibo Lin, Pengpeng Ren, Runsheng Wang, Weikang Qian (2026) (*Equal Contribution)

IEEE/ACM International Conference on Computer-Aided Design (ICCAD) 2026

GUALO is an uncertainty-aware AI agent for logic synthesis recipe search. It uses cross-modal contrastive learning to capture circuit-recipe semantic affinities and decomposes predictive uncertainty into aleatoric and epistemic components, using a lower confidence bound to guide design space exploration. It improves prediction accuracy by up to 17.53% for area and 5.88% for delay, and achieves 6.05% greater average QoR improvement over existing AI-assisted optimization frameworks.

Uncertainty Quantification Logic Optimization Trustworthy AI

Aligning Language Models with Selective Prediction

Gaoxiang Luo, Yifan Wu, Sinian Zhang, Aryan Deshwal, Ju Sun (2026)

Under review, NeurIPS 2026 · arXiv preprint arXiv:2607.03528

This work improves LLM reliability through selective prediction, letting a model answer only when likely correct and defer the rest to human judgment. We propose RLSR (Reinforcement Learning for Selection Reward), an alignment framework that directly targets the area under the risk-coverage curve, achieving a substantially better risk-coverage trade-off than existing alignment baselines on both in-domain and out-of-domain tasks.

Predicting the Timing of First Sustained Cognitive Worsening in Alzheimer's Disease Using Real-World Clinical Data and Machine Learning

Shruthi Venkatesh*, Sinian Zhang*, Wen Zhu, Michele Morris, Rocco Mercurio, Sarah B. Berman, Hansruedi Mathys, Abby L. Olsen, C. Elizabeth Shaaban, Shyam Visweswaran, Oscar L. Lopez, Tianxi Cai, Jue Hou, Zongqi Xia (2026) (*Equal Contribution)

medRxiv preprint

Applying LATTE, a deep neural network for label-efficient incident phenotyping from sparsely labeled longitudinal EHR, we predict time-to-first sustained cognitive worsening across 27,614 Alzheimer's patients. The model achieves robust time-specific AUC and stratifies clinically meaningful subgroups, with APOE-ε4 carriers showing significantly shorter predicted time-to-worsening.

Ensemble Selective Classification

Sinian Zhang, Chongwei Chen, Guanchen Li, Ju Sun

Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS) 2026

The first ensemble framework for post-hoc selective classification. It learns to combine diverse confidence scores from a small labeled calibration set, trained with SoftRank-AURC — a differentiable soft-rank objective for the area under the risk-coverage curve. Across 15 image and NLP tasks it consistently outperforms the best individual score, particularly under distribution shift, without retraining the base classifier.

Selective Classification Uncertainty Quantification Trustworthy AI

Federated Adaptive Causal Estimation with High-Dimensional Covariates (FACE-HD)

Sinian Zhang, Yuanlin Feng, Jue Hou

To be presented at the 7th ICSA-Canada Chapter Symposium, McGill University, Montreal, Aug 2026

A federated framework for estimating average treatment effects when patient-level data are distributed across clinical sites with differing covariate distributions and many candidate confounders. FACE-HD combines penalized nuisance estimation with cross-fitting to form doubly robust source-assisted estimators, aggregating sources via a standardized Wald penalty to guard against non-transportable sites.

Causal Inference Federated Learning Transfer Learning

IntegMultiReg: An R Package for Integrative Bayesian Multi-Regression of Multi-Platform Biomarkers

Sinian Zhang, Jianfeng Wang, Thierry Chekouo

Accompanying R package submitted to CRAN

An R package implementing integrative multi-regression for high-dimensional biomarkers measured across several platforms. It avoids discarding incompletely measured subjects by partitioning them into availability subgroups and borrowing strength through a Markov random field prior, with a Markov chain Monte Carlo sampler written in C.

Bayesian Variable Selection Multi-Omics Statistical Software
